## P1-01: Suppliers, receiving and stock counts

[Approved design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-procurement-and-stock-counts-design.md)
and [implementation plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-16-procurement-and-stock-counts.md).
Scope: one warehouse, admin-only operations; code implementation has not started.

Value: explain where stock came from, its cost and differences from physical
counts. Start with one warehouse.

- Supplier contact records and purchase orders with immutable line snapshots.
- Partial receipts, missing/damaged quantities, and acquisition costs.
- Stock-count sessions with reviewed differences and inventory movements.
- Receiving and approving a count require operator authorization and audit.

Acceptance: receiving the same receipt twice does not double stock; partial
receipts never exceed ordered quantities without an explicit reviewed change;
count approval cannot silently overwrite concurrent sales/reservations.

Dependencies: existing inventory transactions and P0 operator audit patterns.

## P1-02: Serial/IMEI and device warranty

Value: identify the physical device sold and returned.

- Optional per-SKU serial tracking; uniqueness and scan/manual-entry validation.
- Capture serials on receipt and shipment; bind sold devices to order items.
- Warranty history, inspection, repair, replacement and device disposition.

Acceptance: a serial cannot be sold twice; after-sales verifies the device
against its original order; serial count matches quantities for tracked SKUs.
Products without serial tracking retain quantity-based operation.

Dependencies: P1-01 receiving, P0 fulfillment and existing after-sales snapshots.

## P1-03: Staff permissions and store activity audit

Value: separate sales, warehouse, support and financial responsibilities.

- Backend-enforced permissions for each sensitive operation and matching UI.
- Owner-managed staff access and revocation; preserve customer ownership checks.
- Readable, append-only operation history with actor and redacted changes.
- Explicit financial permissions for remittance and refund confirmation.

Acceptance: direct API calls by an unauthorized staff member fail; revocation
takes effect; logs contain neither credentials nor unnecessary personal data.

Dependencies: P0 audit records. Implement before adding operational staff.

## P1-04: Support conversations and assignment

Value: maintain one customer-visible conversation per support case.

- Messages, limited validated attachments and internal/private notes.
- Assigned operator, response deadlines and case history.
- Distinguish messages visible to the customer from internal operational notes.

Acceptance: cross-customer access fails; internal notes never enter customer
responses; attachments obey file-type, size and access restrictions.

Dependencies: existing support ownership, P0 transactional notifications.

## P1-05: Invoicing and gross-profit reporting

Value: issue business documents and measure retained value rather than only
sales volume.

- Invoice recipient details, issuance status and provider integration.
- Cost snapshots, discounts, shipping expenses and refund-aware gross margin.
- Date-bounded reports, exports and invoice-operation history.
- Business invoice policy and provider are selected before implementation.

Acceptance: retries do not issue duplicate invoices; historical cost snapshots
stay stable; reports reconcile to source transactions and distinguish actual
costs from estimates. Missing cost data is shown as unknown, not zero profit.

Dependencies: P1-01 acquisition costs and P0 settlement/refund records.
